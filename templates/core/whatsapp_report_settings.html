Interactive Inbound WhatsApp Commands
Any authorized number above can message the CRM WhatsApp bot at any time to get immediate responses:
WhatsApp Message Bot Action & Response
/report or today Sends real-time today's CRM leads, invoice totals, app orders, live punch-in & break counts.
yesterday or last day Sends yesterday's final figures, revenue, order totals, and employee attendance summary.
overall or all Sends all-time metrics across CRM customers, orders, grand sales volume, and staff stats.
week or this week Sends figures from Monday to the current moment of the active week.
month or this month Sends month-to-date performance across sales and customer acquisition.
custom 01-10-2026 to 10-10-2026 Calculates exact figures for any custom date range (supports DD-MM-YYYY or YYYY-MM-DD).
staff or team Sends detailed per-employee scorecard (calls made, profiles updated, follow-ups due).
help or /help Sends a clean quick-reference menu of all commands directly on WhatsApp.
Automated 6:00 AM Cron Setup
How to ensure the morning report dispatches automatically at 6:00 AM IST.
Server Crontab (crontab -e)

Add these lines to your server's crontab (crontab -e) for automated morning & evening reports:

# 6:00 AM IST — Morning Live Report
0 6 * * * /bin/bash /var/www/apnifactory-crm/scripts/cron_send_morning_report.sh
# 8:00 PM IST — Evening End-of-Day Report
0 20 * * * /bin/bash /var/www/apnifactory-crm/scripts/cron_send_evening_report.sh
Celery Beat (Optional)

Configured in ApniFactoryCRM/settings.py:

'send-morning-dashboard-report-6am': {
  'task': 'core.tasks.send_morning_dashboard_report',
  'schedule': crontab(hour=6, minute=0),
}
Meta Cloud API 24-Hour Policy Note

When an admin sends /report, yesterday, or any message, WhatsApp opens a 24-hour service conversation window. Inside this window, free-form rich reports are delivered instantly. For automated 6:00 AM reports outside the 24h window, standard utility templates can be registered in Meta Business Manager if required.
