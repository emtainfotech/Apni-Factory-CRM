Smart Field Guidelines
Required Field
Mobile / Phone Number

The only strictly required field. Accepts 10-digit Indian numbers, with or without +91, 0, dashes, spaces, or Excel float formatting (.0).

Location Fields (Optional)
City, State & Pincode

Auto-detected from column headers like City, Town, District, State, Province, Pincode, PIN, Zip.

Non-Mandatory Fields
Name, Email & Address

Blank names, missing addresses, and placeholder emails (such as -, N/A, None) are accepted gracefully.

Auto De-duplication Rule:

If a mobile number already exists in your CRM database or appears multiple times within the file, the first instance is preserved and extra duplicates are cleanly skipped.
